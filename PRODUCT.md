# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers managing multiple coding agents and persona reviews from Dispatch's browser chat, including compact panes and mobile screens.

## Product Purpose

Dispatch is a local-first control plane for running and managing AI coding agents. Users need to follow progress, send instructions, inspect reviews, and recover stalled sessions without losing their work.

## Brand Personality

Preserve the existing Dispatch UI and its concise, practical language. This direction was confirmed for the harness queue work on 2026-09-11.

## Anti-references

Avoid a new visual style or a redesign of the surrounding chat. Avoid unbounded notification lists that displace the conversation or composer.

## Design Principles

- Keep the conversation and composer usable as background messages accumulate.
- Disclose detail progressively using the existing component vocabulary.
- Distinguish queued delivery from active work and confirmed completion.
- Keep recovery actions visible and describe their effects accurately.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve existing keyboard, focus, theme, and touch behavior. Expandable queue controls need accessible names and state; overflow must remain scrollable by keyboard and touch.
